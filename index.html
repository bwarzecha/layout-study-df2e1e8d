Important limitations

  • The complete first-floor interior is calibrated to the marketing plan and its labeled room sizes, with an assumed 9' ceiling.
  • All principal wall runs, exterior windows, door openings, stairs and the kitchen footprint follow the plan; fine trim and construction thicknesses remain estimated.
  • The open kitchen scheme removes the dining-wall cabinetry. Its optional 3'6" x 5'6" island is a circulation study, not a finished cabinet or appliance plan.
  • The kitchen beam and post toggles show alternative appearances only. A structural engineer must determine whether the wall is bearing and design any required framing, bearing, foundations and connections.
  • Bench, closet and bathroom fixtures are conceptual scaled objects, not selected products.
  • Option 3 is a nominal 3' x 5' external compact powder room retaining the hall doors. Options 5 and 6 enlarge the side-wall powder room; Options 7 and 8 share a full-width north-end half bath.
  • This is a perspective and massing tool, not measured CAD or a construction drawing.

USING THE MODEL

Compare massing, not finishes

  1. Switch among Options 5 through 8 to compare the two side-wall powder rooms and two north-bath mudroom layouts.
  2. Start with From front door to judge the new opening, then use Looking away from den to compare the modeled foyer with Photo 12.
  3. Use At den opening and Inside den to judge the future mudroom and window relationship.
  4. Trackpad controls: two-finger swipe to pan, Shift-two-finger swipe to orbit, pinch to zoom, and click-drag to orbit.
  5. Mouse controls remain Fusion-style: middle-drag to pan and Shift-middle-drag to orbit.
  6. Drag or click the ViewCube at the right edge, or click its Home button to return immediately to the main front-door view.
  7. Turn on Bathroom cutaway to understand the fixture orientation, or close the bathroom door to block the view through to the retained window.
  8. Enable Existing-wall ghosts to restore the former den-to-foyer wall in Options 5-8. Option 3 already shows the retained wall and doors as solid construction.
  9. Use Save current view to download a PNG for discussion.

The dark-gray translucent panels mark existing door and wall geometry removed or closed by the active option.
